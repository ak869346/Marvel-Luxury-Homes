Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about the product and billing.

+

Where can I find the offer of villa and service prices?

You can find the offers and service prices on the villa's details page under the "Pricing" section.
+

What is included in the price of Accommodation?

The price includes lodging, utility costs, cleaning fees, and listed amenities unless stated otherwise.
+

What is the procedure for arranging and booking Accommodation?

You can book through our website by selecting your preferred villa, dates, and completing the online payment.
+

How do I pay the rest of the Accommodation price, at once or in several installments?

We offer flexible payment options — you can pay the remaining balance either all at once or in two installments.
+

What if I am late with paying the advance payment or the remaining amount?

Late payments may lead to cancellation of your reservation. Please contact us in case of delays to avoid issues.
+

How can I cancel the Accommodation after having paid the advance payment or the remaining amount?

You can cancel your booking by logging into your account and visiting the 'My Bookings' section or contacting support.
+

Will I receive a refund of the advance payment or the remaining amount for the Accommodation in case of cancellation?

Refunds depend on the cancellation policy you agreed to while booking. Check the terms or contact our support team.
+

What are the terms of payment by bank transfer and can I pay by credit card?

We accept bank transfers and all major credit cards. Payment instructions are shared at checkout.
+

Is it possible to rent the villa outside the Saturday to Saturday period?

Yes, some villas offer flexible check-in/check-out. Please check each villa's availability calendar.
+

Is a deposit required?

Most bookings require a security deposit, which is refunded after your stay if no damages are found.
+

Is it possible to check in before the time period?

Early check-in is subject to availability. Contact us ahead of time and we’ll try our best to accommodate.
+

Is it cheaper to book directly through the owner?

Booking through our platform guarantees verified listings and secure transactions with no hidden fees.
