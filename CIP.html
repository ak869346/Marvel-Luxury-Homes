Program Overview

The Antigua & Barbuda Citizenship by Investment Program (CIP) allows eligible investors to secure second citizenship through qualifying real estate investments.

Why Consider Citizenship:

Gaining an Antigua and Barbuda passport through CIP allows for an investor and their immediate family to benefit from full citizenship benefits. As a member state of the British Commonwealth, Antigua and Barbuda enjoys a privileged position for international travel and trade. Passport holders can enjoy visa-free travel to over 140 countries including the United Kingdom, and the Schengen zone. It is a distinct advantage to those with vested interests overseas.

  • Visa-free travel to 140+ countries
  • No inheritance, wealth, or capital gains tax
  • Citizenship for spouse, children, parents
  • Fast-track process with lifelong benefits
